# Product requirements (overview)

An e-commerce business selling used electronics, focused on cameras and high-end audio, runs an AI agent that turns one product photo into a buy/pass verdict, an authenticity risk score and a ready-to-approve listing. A human approves every purchase and every listing.

The project was run like a program: a full PRD with goals and non-goals, success metrics with measured baselines, a risk register, an evaluation plan with a launch gate, and milestones. Only measured results are reported. Targets are never presented as results.

The full PRD is available on request.
